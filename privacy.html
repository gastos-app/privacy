Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Creator Publisher is a personal desktop tool used by its owner to publish his own original videos to his own TikTok accounts. It is not offered to the public and has a single user: the owner.

Data we access

When the owner signs in with TikTok (Login Kit), the app receives an access token and a refresh token for the authorized account, and the account's open_id. These are used only to publish the owner's own videos (scopes user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish).

How data is stored

Tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer. They are not uploaded to any server, sold, shared or used for advertising.

Other users' data

The app does not collect, read or store data from any other TikTok user, and does not post on behalf of third parties.

Retention and deletion

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time from TikTok (Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services) and delete the local tokens at any moment.
